Who is Asim Yousafzai?

Asim Yousafzai (full name Asim Khan Yousafzai, also known as AK, AK Yousafzai and Asim Khan) is the founder and creative director of Con-Gen Media, a full-service digital media and marketing agency that works with brands, businesses and creators around the world.

He started Con-Gen Media to give clients one creative team for everything they publish: short-form reels, long-form YouTube videos, motion graphics, carousels, stories and ads, all built to stop the scroll and turn viewers into customers.

What he has built

  • Founded Con-Gen Media, a digital media and marketing agency serving clients globally.
  • Leads creative direction for reels, Shorts and TikTok edits made for retention and reach.
  • Runs full-length YouTube editing: chapters, captions, thumbnails and Shorts cut-downs.
  • Specialises in motion graphics and animation in Adobe After Effects.
  • Produces campaign and appeal videos for charities and non-profits.
  • Built the Con-Gen free creator toolkit: a sound-effects kit, safe-zone overlays, hook and caption templates.

Career timeline

  • 2018 – present: Video editor and videographer in Lahore, Pakistan.
  • 2022: Featured in the Your Everyday Heroes documentary “Asim Khan ‘AK’ | Parkour – Never Stop Chasing Your Dreams” as a self-taught freerunner, stunt performer and taekwondo medallist (read the feature).
  • 2023 – 2025: Graphic and multimedia designer in Dubai, United Arab Emirates.
  • 2024 – present: Founder & creative director of Con-Gen Media.

Parkour, stunts and martial arts

Before building Con-Gen Media, AK became known as a self-taught parkour and freerunning athlete in Pakistan. He has competed in taekwondo and kickboxing, trained young fighters, and worked as a stunt performer and action coordinator on Pakistani commercials and a feature film.
