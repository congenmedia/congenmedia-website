Who is Asim Yousafzai?

Asim Yousafzai (born Muhammad Asim, also known as AK Yousafzai) is the founder and creative director of Con-Gen Media, a full-service digital media and marketing agency that works with brands, businesses and creators around the world.

He started Con-Gen Media to give clients one creative team for everything they publish: short-form reels, long-form YouTube videos, motion graphics, carousels, stories and ads, all built to stop the scroll and turn viewers into customers.

What he has built

  • Founded Con-Gen Media, a digital media and marketing agency serving clients globally.
  • Leads creative direction for reels, Shorts and TikTok edits made for retention and reach.
  • Runs full-length YouTube editing: chapters, captions, thumbnails and Shorts cut-downs.
  • Specialises in motion graphics and animation in Adobe After Effects.
  • Produces campaign and appeal videos for charities and non-profits.
  • Built the Con-Gen free creator toolkit: a sound-effects kit, safe-zone overlays, hook and caption templates.
